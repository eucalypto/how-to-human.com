👹 Emotions can grow to be overwhelming. They do that mostly when you’re not paying attention to them. Then they can get big and scary.

🔬But similar to illusions, when you start to observe them with curiosity, they start to lose their edges. They won’t disappear completely, but that’s OK, they are valid and valuable. But when you have a clearer picture of your emotions, you can better decide what to do next.
