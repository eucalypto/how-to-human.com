Let things be is how you let things go. You don't let them go and push them away as far as possible. 

you can let go and stay near. Let it go, stay near, observe and be curious at how it will develop if you let it be, give it room to breathe.

You may fear that if you let go, you will be swept away from it and never find it again. This fear is real but unfounded. I invite you to be open to the possibility that if you let it go, it will just float in front of you and you can grab onto it at any time if you like.
